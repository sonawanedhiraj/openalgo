# R60 — July 2026 after the #502 data-sourcing fix

**Issue:** [#502](https://github.com/sonawanedhiraj/openalgo/issues/502) ·
**Harness:** [`backtest/options_open15/july_502_run.py`](../../../../backtest/options_open15/july_502_run.py) ·
**Window:** 2026-07-01 → 2026-07-31 (19 trading days with signals) ·
**Date:** 2026-07-31

## What this round answers

The #502 audit found three bugs in the LIVE strategy, all from sourcing the
09:15 first candle from a ~1/sec tick sample instead of the broker's settled
bar. Only one of them is expressible in a bar-based backtest:

| Bug | Lives in | In a bar backtest? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — 09:15 open = first tick received, not the day open | live only | **No** — a bar replay already reads the broker's settled open |
| 2 — tick-built H1/L1 narrower than the real candle | live only | **No** — same reason |
| 3 — 09:15 minute (+ pre-open auction) inflating the volume baseline | live **and** backtest | **Yes** |

So R59 was already running the *fixed* data sourcing; production was not. This
round isolates bug 3: **what does dropping the inflated baseline do to July?**

## Reading it honestly

- **The fix does not create edge — it stops throwing signals away.** The gate
  was configured at 1.5× and behaving like ~2.5×. Correcting it roughly doubles
  the signal count. Per-trade quality goes DOWN (win rate −5.5pp, Sharpe 6.33 →
  4.32 on the stock leg); the month improves because there are more of them.
- **Trade count is still tiny.** 30 signals in a month, one month of data. The
  R58 full-history finding — the honest bar-level edge is **−0.16%/trade** —
  is not overturned by a favourable month at n=30. Treat the parity numbers as
  a deployment-sanity check, not validation.
- **The option leg's 21.46% is concentrated.** Five trades (NATIONALUM +3,768,
  KALYANKJIL +3,396, HYUNDAI +2,714 and +2,268, LODHA +1,643) are ₹13,789 of
  the ₹14,484. Remove them and the month is roughly flat. This is a
  small-sample, fat-tail result.
- **"Unaffordable" is a real outcome, not a data gap.** 10 of 30 fixed-arm
  signals could not be sized into a ₹22,500 slot (premium × lot > slot) and are
  genuine ₹0 days. Exactly **one** trade (NYKAA 2026-07-22) is a true data gap
  — its 25-AUG contract has no cached premium bar.
- **Expired-contract reconstruction.** The 28-JUL-26 series is purged from
  `symtoken`, so `pick_contract` cannot see it. The harness rebuilds those
  contracts from the premium cache (strike/expiry/type parse from the contract
  symbol; lot size from the current `symtoken` row for the same underlying —
  verified identical for all 15 R59 contracts, 15/15). Without this the option
  leg would have been unpriceable for the whole first half of July.

## Decision

Ship the fix (it is a correctness fix — the live strategy was mis-selecting and
mis-gating regardless of what the P&L does). **Do not** re-rate the strategy on
this month. The R58 decision rule stands: the trigger entry must beat the close
entry by ≥0.4pp/trade over ~30+ LIVE signals or the strategy is a final REJECT.
The fix matters mostly because it makes the live measurement *valid* — pre-fix,
the sandbox was measuring a gate that never fired and levels that were not the
real candle's.

> ### ⚠️ SUPERSEDED, same day — read this before quoting anything above
>
> Everything above is a ONE-MONTH result, and July 2026 turned out to be the
> best month of the trailing year. Two later runs on this branch overturn the
> optimistic reading:
>
> 1. **Past year on production rules** (2025-08-01..2026-07-31, 275 signals;
>    harness `year_502_run.py` is LOCAL-ONLY — it builds on the untracked R58
>    `bs.py` / `run_bs_backtest.py`, same status as the R58/R59 harnesses whose
>    results this repo already cites): stock leg **−₹39,471** on ₹180k; per-trade equity return
>    **+0.003%, t=+0.08** — statistically indistinguishable from zero. Only 4 of
>    12 months green. Long > short holds (long +0.033%/trade vs short −0.039%)
>    but orders two ≈zero legs.
> 2. **Tick analysis of the 8 captured days** — the R58 decision rule is now
>    directly measured and **FAILS**: the tick entry beat the bar-close entry by
>    **−0.034pp/trade**, against the ≥ +0.40pp the rule required. By the time the
>    volume gate is satisfied the intra-bar burst is ~107% gone (already-gone
>    +0.515% vs full burst +0.481%, t≈4). The look-ahead gap is confirmed at
>    0.52pp/trade (level entry +0.781% vs tick entry +0.265%).
>
> The option numbers in this doc are also unreliable in both directions: three
> July rows are stale/one-sided prints in thin contracts (~₹15.7k of inflation),
> the 07-27 and 07-28 trades used the wrong expiry (25-AUG instead of the live
> 28-JUL series), and no bid-ask was modelled at all — worth ~₹57k on its own,
> against a longs-only break-even of ~0.41%/side.
>
> **Net: the #502 fix is still correct and worth shipping; the strategy it
> measures is heading for REJECT.** The remaining open question is execution
> cost, which only live bid/ask capture can answer.

## Reproduce

```bash
BT_PICKS_R59=backtest/options_open15/data/picks_0701_0724.json \
BT_PICKS_TAIL=backtest/options_open15/data/picks_0727_0731.json \
BT_OUT=/tmp/july502.json PYTHONPATH=. \
uv run python backtest/options_open15/july_502_run.py
```

Full per-trade dump (both arms): `backtest/options_open15/data/july502_results.json`.

**Note:** `data/july_fetch_cache.json` (the 2.3 MB real-premium cache) is NOT
committed — it exceeds the repo's 1 MB file gate. Without it the option leg
re-fetches, and the expired 28-JUL-26 contracts are unrecoverable, so only the
stock leg reproduces on a fresh clone. The committed results JSON carries every
option number this round reports.
