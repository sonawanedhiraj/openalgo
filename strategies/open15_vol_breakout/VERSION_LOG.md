| 0.1.4 | 2026-08-06 | Observability + measurement only — **no signal, gate, sizing or order change**. (a) **Broker fill reconciliation**: `services/open15_fill_reconcile.py` reads the broker's `average_price` per leg at the summary job (exit+5, retried at the next 09:10 arm) into new `entry_fill_price`/`exit_fill_price` columns and **re-derives `pnl`/`charges_inr` in place** from them, stamping `pnl_source='fill'`. The quote/tick columns are untouched, so `fill − quote` is now the measurable slippage. Charges stay **modelled** (no broker exposes per-order charges via API) but on actual fill turnover, and are labelled as modelled in the UI. `broker_pnl` records the position book's own realized P&L as a cross-check. (b) **Both legs are shown**: in `atm_option` mode the page used to render the stock trigger price next to a P&L computed on the premium; the `entry`/`exit` events and the selection table now carry both, and `armed` records the day's `instrument`. (c) **Simulated non-traded rows**: `unaffordable` / `max_trades_cap` triggers are priced at **1 lot** (`fill='sim'`, `sim_quantity`; `quantity` stays 0) so a capped or under-funded day can say whether the slot capital or the signal limited it. Three P&L buckets — real / paper / sim — reported apart and never summed; `_REAL_FILL` became an explicit exclusion list so sim money can never compound into tomorrow's real position size. Flags `OPEN15_FILL_RECONCILE_ENABLED`, `OPEN15_SIM_SKIPPED_ENABLED`, `OPEN15_PAPER_SIM_MAX`. | Issue #555. 2026-08-06 was the trigger: the day traded ATM options, so `entered @ 2952.9` was the STOCK price beside an option P&L, `opt_entry_premium` was a quote and not a fill (nothing had ever been reconciled against the broker), and three `unaffordable` rows carried a contract and an entry premium and then stopped — no exit, no P&L, no label. Operator decisions locked the same day: three separate buckets, 1-lot sim size, modelled-and-labelled charges. |

| 0.1.3 | 2026-08-03 | Rolling additive watch list, **default OFF** (deploy is a no-op). When enabled, the universe is re-ranked on live LTP every `rolling_cadence_s` (default 30 s, clamped 10–300, **editable from the `/logs` config form** along with `rolling_top_n` and the on/off switch) inside the entry window, and the top-N movers per side are APPENDED to the watch list. Strictly additive — the 09:16 seed picks are never dropped and `watch_size` never shrinks. **The entry gate, the level, the one-entry-per-symbol rule and the `max_trades` cap are all unchanged**; added symbols compete for the same slots first-come-first-served. Each addition emits a `watchlist_add` decision-log event (symbol / side / % change at add / rank / watch size), rendered in a new "Rolling watch-list" panel on `/logs`; the selection-outcome table gains a seed-vs-rolling `source` column, and `open15_trades` gains `watch_source ∈ {seed, rolling}`. | Issue #529. 2026-08-03 replay: the 09:16 gap ranking placed the day's four biggest movers at ranks #22/#106/#130/#134, and two of the three symbols actually selected never entered the intraday top-3. ⚠ **The same study could NOT show the added names pay** — 3 incremental trades, +₹162, 1 win of 3, on the only 4 usable days. Shipped as a MEASUREMENT (hence `watch_source`), not a validated edge; sample size is blocked on #528. |
| 0.1.2 | 2026-08-03 | Observability only — no signal, sizing or gate change. The running max cumvol/baseline ratio is now published while the window is open: `get_status()` gains `watch_stats` + `vol_needed`, a new `watch_stats` decision-log event covers **every** selected symbol at the exit job (entered ones included), and `/logs` fills its `max vol×` column from both. `no_entry` keeps precedence, so pre-#524 stored days and the CSV export parse unchanged. `max_vol_ratio` is `None` (rendered `—`) until a symbol's first in-window tick, so "no ticks" stays distinguishable from a 0.0 ratio. The `needed` threshold now reports the day config's `vol_mult` rather than the raw env default. | Issue #524. The value was already computed tick-by-tick and final at the entry cutoff; it was simply never published until the exit job, leaving the column blank all session and permanently blank for entered symbols. |
